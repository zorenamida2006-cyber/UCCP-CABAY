About Our Church

Getting to know UCCP Cabay - our story, our purpose, and our people.

Our History

The UCCP Cabay Church was established in a community in Barangay Cabay, Balangkayan, Eastern Samar. Among its founders were Mr. and Mrs. Pedro Yadao. Mr. Pedro Yadao was formerly an ex-convict because he had killed a person and was sentenced to two (2) years of imprisonment. After his release from prison following the liberation during the Second World War in 1945, he decided to be baptized at UCCP Balangkayan by Rev. Eutiquio G. Contado, into the Protestant faith under the denomination of UCCP, or the United Church of Christ in the Philippines. From the time of his baptism, he began serving the church. He and his wife, Mr. Pedro Yadao and Mrs. Victoria Yadao, would walk to Balangkayan every Sunday to attend worship services. They faithfully attended worship every Sunday.Since they regularly attended worship in Balangkayan, Mr. Pedro Yadao requested Pastor Eutiquio to conduct an Evangelistic Meeting in Cabay. Pastor Contado granted his request and came with his assistants, Pastor Jorge Montes and Pastor Nathaniel Detalo, who at that time were still students at the seminary.The Evangelistic Meeting they conducted was not in vain because there were people who were convinced and decided to follow the Lord. Among the families who became members of UCCP Cabay were Mr. and Mrs. Juan Yadao, the brother of Pedro Yadao. These two families then worshiped together at UCCP Balangkayan.

In 1949, Ptr. Nathaniel Detalo was assigned to Cabay as a worker for the Gospel of Jesus Christ, our Lord. He stayed at the home of Mr. and Mrs. Juan Yadao. The family created a partition in their house to provide him with a place to stay, while the other side of the partition was used as their place of worship.On the last Sunday of November 1960, the first regular worship service was held at the home of Mr. and Mrs. Juan Yadao. Those who attended included Mr. and Mrs. Telesforo Tazarte, Mr. and Mrs. Crispin Codillo, Mr. Roberto Egoy, Mr. Emeterio Ador, Mrs. Marcelina Clima Glino, Mr. and Mrs. Francisco Contado, and Mr. and Mrs. Inigo Yadao Sr. As their worship services continued and became regular, they planned to build their own church. They decided to build the church on top of the hill, across from the Elementary School and the home of Mr. and Mrs. Yadao.The system they used in building the church was the Pintakasi system. The materials came from coconut lumber, and the roof was also made using the materials they had gathered. After the church was built, the members continued to climb the hill every Sunday to attend worship services.

In 1962, Mr. and Mrs. Eufracio Contado settled in Cabay. Because of this, they planned to celebrate the Church Anniversary. Up to the present, the church continues to celebrate its anniversaries.Despite the great calamity that destroyed the structure of UCCP Cabay, the members continued worshiping under the house of Mr. and Mrs. Eufracio Contado until 1990. The pastor assigned to the church during that time was Pastora Jennylend Contado.The members made efforts to plan for the construction of a new church because many members were already hesitant to worship in the house. Therefore, the congregation decided not to rebuild the church on the hill, where the previous church had stood. The older members requested that the church no longer be built on the hill because their knees had become weak, making it difficult for them to climb to the top. Therefore, everyone agreed to build the church across the road, on the land owned by Mr. and Mrs. Eufracio Contado, in exchange for the land on the hill where UCCP Cabay was first established.

When Mr. and Mrs. Inigo Yadao Jr. returned home, they planned to replace or improve the temporary church. Because of this, the couple, together with the church members, used chainsaws to cut wood that would be used for the posts and walls. The men of the church also helped in the construction.The church that we worship in today is the same church building that they worked to establish. Its architect was Engr. Abner Contado. UCCP Cabay continues to celebrate its church anniversaries.In 1962, Ptr. Nathaniel Detalo left Cabay to complete his studies in Baguio. He was succeeded by Mrs. Easter Gesite as the Bible Woman and Mrs. Concepcion Contado as the Sunday School Teacher.

As the years passed, several pastors were assigned to UCCP Cabay, including Pastora Jenny Lend Contado, Pastora Gracia Baris, Pastor Carlito Elardo, Pastor Paquito M. Rodriguez, Pastor Edencio Gupa, and the present pastor, Rev. Jenny Lend Contado. In 2019, a Satellite of Compassion was established in Cabay. At present, the CDWs who have served are Norlyn Globio, followed by Claudine Alota, who continue to serve as CDWs. When Mr. and Mrs. Jerson Yadao came home for a vacation, they saw that the church needed improvement. Because of their love and support for the church, they planned to provide a budget specifically for the improvement and renovation of the UCCP-Cabay building.Because of this support, the UCCP Cabay Church now has its improved appearance. The church continues to be improved and maintained, and the person serving the church during this period is Rev. Edencio M. Gupa.

Mission & Vision

What guides everything we do as a church family.

Our Mission

The UCCP commits itself to establishing and uniting communities of faith for the proclamation of the Gospel of our Lord Jesus Christ, working toward the transformation of both the church and society.

Our Vision

The United Church of Christ in the Philippines (UCCP) envisions a responsible, empowered, self-reliant, and caring community of Christian believers, committed to transforming the church and society toward an abundant and meaningful life for all.

Goal

Within the first 5–10 years of its Jubilee, the UCCP seeks to translate its mission into a life-work focused on the restoration of relationships—a foretaste of God's reign and shalom. Strengthen the Faith Community Enrich the Life-Work of Communities where Local Churches are located. Deepen the Impact of Collective Responses to societal issues and concerns.
